# Keep the live scoring loop visible on phones and tablets

Live Court keeps player selection, Rally Outcomes, Dig, the latest-action receipt, and Undo visible together during routine scoring. A scorer must be able to record ten consecutive rallies without scrolling. Phone acceptance includes 320 × 568, 375 × 667, and 390 × 844 portrait viewports. Landscape tablets retain simultaneous court and scoring visibility at 1024 × 768.

Phones use a compact scoreboard and six player cards in court order, with scoring controls immediately below. More opens secondary panels for the expanded court, substitutions, statistics, and match controls. Secondary panels and set setup may scroll. Touch targets remain at least 44px high.

Ace and Service error continue to attribute automatically to the P1 server. Those controls identify the server explicitly, including when another player is selected. Other player-specific actions require selection, and a successful recorded action clears that selection.

This decision replaces the earlier allowance for scrolling between player selection and scoring on phones.
